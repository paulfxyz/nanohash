02 Size it for your application

MATH, NOT A SECURITY SCORE

A little longer.
A lot more room.

Every extra character multiplies the space by the size of your alphabet. Pick a length for your expected volume, not by appearance alone.

SUGGESTED LENGTH

11 characters meet this collision budget.
